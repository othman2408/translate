## Design Context

### Users
People using the extension while reading normal web pages in Chrome, Edge, or Firefox. They need a fast way to translate selected text without leaving the page, and they will mostly configure the extension once, then expect it to stay quiet.

### Brand Personality
Calm, precise, compact. The UI should feel trustworthy and lightweight, with a personal-tool polish rather than a commercial dashboard tone.

### Aesthetic Direction
MacOS-inspired, not copied: grouped settings, soft separators, system-like controls, restrained Apple-inspired colors, and smooth push navigation for deeper settings. The popup should remain extension-sized and focused, not become a full settings dashboard.

### Design Principles
- Keep the first screen compact and scannable.
- Group settings by user intent: translation, interaction, provider, and data.
- Use typography as hierarchy: clear titles, quiet metadata, readable control labels.
- Prefer restrained system color and spacing tokens over decorative effects.
- Preserve RTL and mixed-direction translation readability as a core product quality.
